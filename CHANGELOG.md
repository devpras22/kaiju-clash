# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — 2026-09-23
- First playable build. Includes procedural Godzilla and King Kong models, a destructible night city, full combat with specials and supers, a CPU opponent in three difficulties, local versus, a fighting-game UI, and procedural audio.
- Known issues: the monsters render too dark, the camera gets too close when fighters clinch, and balance favours Godzilla.
